VSCO 2 CE chapel organ (C3–C5 excerpt, loud/open)
================================================

Original recordings: Simon Dalzell (Ivy Audio), winter 2015–16
Library: Versilian Studios Chamber Orchestra 2 Community Edition
https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE
SFZ mapping: OrganLoud.sfz (Rode NT5 spaced pair, manual 3 open)

Full text: LICENSE in this folder

These files are a C3–C5 excerpt of the loud/open layer. Notes that
were not originally sampled (every minor third) were pitch-shifted
by at most one semitone and converted to 44.1 kHz 16-bit mono WAV.

Dalzell / Ivy Audio and Versilian Studios.
