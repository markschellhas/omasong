Orgue Eglise (C3–C5 excerpt, Full stop)
=======================================

Original GigaSampler library: S. Christian Collins
https://www.schristiancollins.com

SFZ conversion: https://github.com/sfzinstruments/OrgueEglise
Preset used: Orgue Eglise Full

These files are a C3–C5 excerpt of the Full stop. Notes that were not
originally sampled (C, E, G per octave) were pitch-shifted by at most
two semitones and converted to 44.1 kHz 16-bit mono WAV.

The upstream GitHub conversion does not include a license file. Credit
S. Christian Collins if you ship this excerpt.
