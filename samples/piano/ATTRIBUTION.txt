Salamander Grand Piano (C3–C5 excerpt)
======================================

Original recordings: Alexander Holm — Salamander Grand Piano V3
Yamaha C5, 48 kHz / 24-bit, two AKG C414
Source: https://archive.org/details/SalamanderGrandPianoV3
SFZ mapping: https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SalamanderGrandPiano

Full text: LICENSE in this folder

These files are a one-velocity (layer v8) excerpt covering C3–C5.
Notes that were not originally sampled (Salamander is sampled every
minor third) were pitch-shifted by at most one semitone and converted
to 44.1 kHz 16-bit stereo WAV.

If you ship this, keep this ATTRIBUTION file and LICENSE with the
samples, and credit Alexander Holm.
